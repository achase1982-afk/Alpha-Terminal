STEP 1 BASE CONFIDENCE FROM COMPOSITE MAGNITUDE. The base confidence is a linear scale of how far the composite is from zero. The composite theoretical range is negative 2.0 to positive 2.0 but in practice rarely exceeds 1.0. Use the formula:

baseConfidence = min(95, round(abs(composite) * 120))

This produces: composite 0.15 gives base 18. Composite 0.30 gives base 36. Composite 0.50 gives base 60. Composite 0.80 gives base 95 (capped). Composite 1.0 gives base 95 (capped). The cap at 95 means confidence never reaches 100 because there is always some uncertainty.

STEP 2 AGREEMENT MULTIPLIER. Count how many clusters are opposing the composite direction. A cluster is opposing if the composite is positive and the cluster score is below negative 0.5, or if the composite is negative and the cluster score is above positive 0.5. Clusters between negative 0.5 and positive 0.5 are not considered opposing regardless of sign.

opposingCount = number of clusters that oppose the composite direction using the 0.5 threshold above

agreementMultiplier = max(0.4, 1.0 - (opposingCount * 0.15))

This produces: 0 opposing gives multiplier 1.0. 1 opposing gives 0.85. 2 opposing gives 0.70. 3 opposing gives 0.55. 4 or more opposing gives 0.40 (floor). The floor of 0.4 prevents confidence from being completely destroyed even in maximum conflict scenarios.

STEP 3 DATA QUALITY MULTIPLIER. Keep the existing staleness and data quality penalties but floor the combined data quality multiplier at 0.5. If there are no staleness or data quality issues the multiplier is 1.0.

STEP 4 FINAL CONFIDENCE. Multiply the three factors together:

confidence = round(baseConfidence * agreementMultiplier * dataQualityMultiplier)
confidence = max(0, min(100, confidence))
