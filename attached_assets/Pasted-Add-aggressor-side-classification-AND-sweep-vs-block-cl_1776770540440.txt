Add aggressor side classification AND sweep vs block classification to the Unusual Options scanner as core detection dimensions. These two together are what actually distinguishes institutional positioning from noise.

Implementation:

1. For every unusual strike detected, classify each trade print by aggressor side:
   - Print price >= ask at time of print: AGGRESSIVE BUY (bullish for calls, bearish for puts)
   - Print price <= bid at time of print: AGGRESSIVE SELL (bearish for calls, bullish for puts)
   - Print price between bid and ask: NEUTRAL

2. For every unusual strike, also classify trades by execution pattern:
   - SWEEP: same contract, 3+ prints across 2+ exchanges within 500ms, total size >= 100 contracts. Signals urgency, typically aggressive institutional algo slicing a large order to avoid alerting the market.
   - BLOCK: single print >= 500 contracts OR >= 10% of contract OI. Signals a negotiated institutional position, often through dark pools or block trading desks.
   - REGULAR: everything else (retail or non-urgent fills).

3. For each detected unusual strike, compute:
   - Ask-side volume and notional (aggressive buys)
   - Bid-side volume and notional (aggressive sells)
   - Neutral volume
   - Sweep count and sweep notional
   - Block count and block notional
   - Dominant aggressor side with 70% threshold: BULLISH, BEARISH, or MIXED
   - Execution pattern summary: e.g. "3 sweeps, 1 block, 12 regular"

4. Aggregate per-ticker across all unusual strikes: overall direction, total sweep notional, total block notional, total regular notional.

5. Scoring adjustment: directional aggression combined with sweep or block execution gets full credit. Mixed/neutral flow or purely regular execution gets reduced credit because the signal is less clean.

6. Card display: show aggressor split AND execution pattern on each row. Example:
