ADDENDUM TO STRATEGIST v2 SPEC: SETTINGS PANEL + TELEMETRY

Ship this alongside the main Strategist v2 spec. Every tunable parameter becomes a setting in the app. Every decision the scanner and Strategist make gets logged in detail. Nothing is hardcoded. Everything is adjustable and auditable.

# ==========================================================
PART 1: SCANNER & STRATEGIST SETTINGS PANEL

Create a new settings page accessible from the app settings. Title: “Scanner & Strategist Tuning.” Group the settings into the sections below. Every setting has a label, current value, input type, default, and allowed range. Store all values in the database so they persist across restarts and environments.

— IDIOSYNCRATIC EDGE SCORE (IOScore) —

IOScore Weight: Market Independence (R²)
Default: 0.30 | Range: 0.00 to 0.50 | Step: 0.05
What it does: How much weight the “is this stock independent from SPY” factor gets.

IOScore Weight: Abnormal Move (Residual Return)
Default: 0.25 | Range: 0.00 to 0.50 | Step: 0.05
What it does: How much weight the “is this stock making an unusual move” factor gets.

IOScore Weight: Catalyst (Benzinga)
Default: 0.25 | Range: 0.00 to 0.50 | Step: 0.05
What it does: How much weight the “does this stock have earnings/analyst news” factor gets.

IOScore Weight: Options Flow (Polygon)
Default: 0.20 | Range: 0.00 to 0.50 | Step: 0.05
What it does: How much weight the “are options traders doing something unusual” factor gets.

Note: Display a warning if all four weights do not sum to 1.0. Auto-normalize if the user adjusts one.

IOScore Threshold: High Idiosyncratic
Default: 0.65 | Range: 0.40 to 0.90 | Step: 0.05
What it does: Above this score, the Strategist follows the stock’s own direction regardless of macro regime.

IOScore Threshold: Mixed Floor
Default: 0.40 | Range: 0.20 to 0.60 | Step: 0.05
What it does: Below this score, the stock is considered macro-aligned. Between this and the high threshold is “mixed.”

Beta/R² Lookback Window
Default: 30 days | Range: 10 to 60 | Step: 5
What it does: How many trading days of history to use when calculating how closely the stock tracks SPY.

Residual Return Lookback
Default: 10 days | Range: 3 to 20 | Step: 1
What it does: How many days of recent price action to compare against expected beta move.

— SCANNER WEIGHTS —

Default Mode (Trending Market):

Trend Weight
Default: 25 | Range: 0 to 40 | Step: 5

Relative Strength Weight
Default: 20 | Range: 0 to 40 | Step: 5

Volume Weight
Default: 20 | Range: 0 to 40 | Step: 5

IVR Weight
Default: 20 | Range: 0 to 40 | Step: 5

Options Liquidity Weight
Default: 15 | Range: 0 to 40 | Step: 5

Idiosyncratic Mode (NEUTRAL/TRANSITION Market):

Trend Weight
Default: 15 | Range: 0 to 40 | Step: 5

Relative Strength vs SPY Weight
Default: 25 | Range: 0 to 40 | Step: 5

Relative Volume Weight
Default: 25 | Range: 0 to 40 | Step: 5

IVR Weight
Default: 20 | Range: 0 to 40 | Step: 5

Options Liquidity Weight
Default: 15 | Range: 0 to 40 | Step: 5

Catalyst Bonus Points
Default: 10 | Range: 0 to 25 | Step: 1
What it does: Flat score boost for tickers with confirmed catalyst (earnings, analyst actions + unusual flow). Applied only in idiosyncratic mode. Clamped at max score 100.

Scanner Minimum Score Threshold
Default: 55 | Range: 30 to 80 | Step: 5
What it does: Minimum composite score to appear in scan results.

— STRATEGY SELECTION —

IVR Credit/Debit Threshold
Default: 40 | Range: 20 to 60 | Step: 5
What it does: Above this IVR, Strategist prefers credit spreads (sell premium). Below, prefers debit spreads (buy premium).

Preferred DTE Range: Minimum
Default: 30 | Range: 7 to 45 | Step: 1

Preferred DTE Range: Maximum
Default: 60 | Range: 30 to 90 | Step: 5

Spread Width
Default: $5 | Range: $1 to $10 | Step: 0.50

Minimum Open Interest Per Leg
Default: 50 | Range: 10 to 500 | Step: 10

Maximum Bid-Ask Spread (% of mid)
Default: 25% | Range: 5% to 50% | Step: 5%

— EARNINGS GATE —

Earnings Proximity Window
Default: 48 hours | Range: 0 to 96 | Step: 12
What it does: How close to earnings the Strategist applies special rules. Set to 0 to disable earnings gate entirely.

Earnings IVR Floor for Credit Spreads
Default: 70 | Range: 40 to 90 | Step: 5
What it does: Within the earnings window, credit spreads only allowed if IVR is above this level. Otherwise forced to debit or longer DTE.

— CORRELATION REGIME —

Correlation Threshold: LOW ceiling
Default: 0.40 | Range: 0.20 to 0.50 | Step: 0.05

Correlation Threshold: HIGH floor
Default: 0.75 | Range: 0.60 to 0.90 | Step: 0.05

— TOXIC DAY GATE —

Toxic Gate Enabled
Default: ON | Toggle
What it does: Master switch for the toxic day gate. Turn off to never block any day.

Toxic Path A: Require EXTREME Risk + HIGH Correlation
Default: ON | Toggle

Toxic Path B: Major Event within 24h + ELEVATED Risk
Default: ON | Toggle

Major Events List
Default: FOMC Rate Decision, CPI Print | Editable list
What it does: Which Benzinga economic calendar events trigger Path B. Add or remove events.

— REGIME —

Regime Update Frequency
Default: 5 minutes | Range: 1 to 30 | Step: 1

— RESET —

Reset All to Defaults button that restores every setting to the defaults listed above.

PART 2: TELEMETRY AND AUDIT LOGGING

Every scanner run and every Strategist analysis logs a detailed record to a telemetry table. These are viewable in a new “Telemetry” tab in the app settings or in a dedicated Telemetry page.

— SCANNER TELEMETRY —

Every scan writes one record:

{
type: “SCANNER_RUN”,
timestamp: ISO-8601,
regime: { full regime object },
mode: “DEFAULT” or “IDIOSYNCRATIC”,
weights_used: { trend: X, rs: X, volume: X, ivr: X, liquidity: X },
universe_size: number,
passed_filters: number,
above_threshold: number,
threshold_used: number,
catalyst_bonus_applied_to: [list of tickers that received bonus],
results: [
{
ticker: “AAPL”,
raw_score: 62,
catalyst_bonus: 10,
final_score: 72,
sub_scores: { trend: 8, rs: 18, volume: 15, ivr: 16, liquidity: 12 },
filtered_out: false,
filter_reason: null
},
{
ticker: “BAC”,
raw_score: null,
filtered_out: true,
filter_reason: “No options data”
}
]
}

Log ALL tickers, not just the ones that passed. This lets you see why specific tickers were filtered or scored low.

— TELEMETRY VIEWING —

Build a simple Telemetry page with:

1. A table of recent Strategist analyses (last 50). Columns: timestamp, ticker, idioStrength, strategy, direction, result (recommended / no viable setup / toxic block).
1. Tap any row to expand the full telemetry record showing every field listed above.
1. A table of recent Scanner runs (last 20). Columns: timestamp, mode, regime, tickers scanned, above threshold, top result.
1. Tap any scanner row to expand the full record with all ticker scores.
1. A simple filter: by date, by ticker, by result type.

— TELEMETRY RETENTION —

Keep telemetry records for 30 days. After 30 days, auto-delete. This prevents the table from growing forever.

SUMMARY

This addendum creates two things:

1. A settings panel with every tunable parameter exposed. No more prompting Replit to change a hardcoded number. Adjust the IOScore threshold from 0.65 to 0.72 with a slider. Change the scanner weights. Toggle the toxic gate on or off. Widen or narrow the earnings window. All stored in the database, persisted across restarts.
1. A telemetry system that logs every decision in full detail. When a scan returns unexpected results or the Strategist makes a recommendation you question, open the telemetry and read the entire decision path. No more guessing why something happened.

Ship this with the main Strategist v2 spec. They are one unit.
