FIX: Iron condor not detected, max risk and downstream fields miscalculated on 4-leg credit spreads

SYMPTOM (repro: INTC May 8 2026, long 55P / short 60P / short 70C / long 75C, net credit 2.46):
- Max risk displays 1754 (wrong)
- Buying Power displays 246 (wrong)
- Breakeven displays blank (wrong)
- R:R displays 0.1:1 (wrong)
- Risk Overview margin displays 24754 (wrong)

EXPECTED for same ticket:
- Max risk: 254 (max wing width 5.00 minus net credit 2.46, times 100)
- Buying Power: 254
- Breakeven: 57.54 / 72.46 (lower short strike minus credit, upper short strike plus credit)
- R:R: 0.97:1
- Margin: 254

ROOT CAUSE:
Strategy detector is not tagging this leg combination as IRON_CONDOR before risk math runs. Risk math is falling through to a generic path that treats the 55-to-75 span as a single loss envelope (20.00 wide minus 2.46 credit = 17.54 per share = 1754). An iron condor can only lose on one wing at expiration, so max loss is max(put_wing_width, call_wing_width) minus net credit.

DETECTION RULE for IRON_CONDOR:
- Exactly 4 legs
- Same underlying, same expiration across all legs
- Two puts: one long, one short, short strike > long strike
- Two calls: one long, one short, short strike < long strike
- Short put strike < short call strike (i.e. short strikes inside long strikes)
- Net credit (sum of shorts mid > sum of longs mid)

RISK MATH for IRON_CONDOR:
- put_wing_width = short_put_strike - long_put_strike
- call_wing_width = long_call_strike - short_call_strike
- max_risk_per_share = max(put_wing_width, call_wing_width) - net_credit_per_share
- max_risk_dollars = max_risk_per_share * 100 * quantity
- buying_power = max_risk_dollars
- breakeven_lower = short_put_strike - net_credit_per_share
- breakeven_upper = short_call_strike + net_credit_per_share
- max_profit_dollars = net_credit_per_share * 100 * quantity
- r_r_ratio = max_profit_dollars / max_risk_dollars

SCOPE:
