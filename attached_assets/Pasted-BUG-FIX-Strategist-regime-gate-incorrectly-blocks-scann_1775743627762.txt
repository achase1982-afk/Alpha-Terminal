BUG FIX: Strategist regime gate incorrectly blocks scanner micro-override tickers
PROBLEM: When a ticker is sent from the Scanner to the Strategist via the Send to Strategist button, and that ticker has a micro-override flag (meaning its stock-level technicals scored high enough to override weak macro conditions), the Strategist still runs its Pulse regime gate check and rejects the ticker with “No actionable regime detected. The scoring engine shows insufficient conviction to recommend a trade. Sit this one out.” This is wrong. The entire point of the micro-override feature is that the stock’s individual strength is enough to trade despite a weak or neutral macro Pulse reading.
WHAT SHOULD HAPPEN: When the Strategist receives a ticker with source equal to scanner AND microOverride equal to true, it must completely bypass the Pulse regime gate and proceed directly into strategy selection. The Scanner has already validated edge through hard scoring across Trend, Relative Strength, Volume, IVR, and Options Liquidity. The Strategist should trust that pre-screening and focus only on picking the right options strategy and strikes for the ticker.
WHERE TO LOOK: Find the Strategist analysis pipeline, specifically the function or block that checks the Pulse composite score and confidence to determine if there is an actionable regime. This is the gate that produces the “No actionable regime detected” rejection message. The fix goes immediately before that gate.
THE FIX: Add a conditional check before the regime gate. If source is scanner AND microOverride is true, skip the regime gate entirely. Let the analysis continue into strategy selection using the score breakdown data that was passed from the Scanner, including the individual category scores for trend, relative strength, volume, IVR, and options liquidity. Also pass through the bias from the Pulse (even though we are overriding the regime gate, the bias direction is still useful context for the AI narrative layer when selecting bullish vs bearish vs neutral strategies).
IMPORTANT: Do not remove or weaken the regime gate itself. It must still function normally for manually navigated tickers where the user typed a symbol directly into the Strategist without going through the Scanner. The bypass only applies when both conditions are true: source is scanner AND microOverride is true.
VERIFY: After the fix, sending INTC from the Scanner with a micro-override flag and a score of 94 to the Strategist should produce a full trade recommendation, not a rejection, even when the Pulse composite is negative and confidence is low.
