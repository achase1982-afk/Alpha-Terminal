Position size recommendation which is FULL or HALF based on the mode. Profit target as a percentage of credit received, defaulting to 50 percent. Stop loss as a multiple of credit received, defaulting to 2x. Maximum number of contracts based on the portfolio exposure cap of 30 percent aggregate risk, checking current open positions.
