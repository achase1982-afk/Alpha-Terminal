REDESIGN: Strategist Output Page Layout
Reference the attached mockup artifact (strategist-mockup.jsx). The current Strategist output page needs to match this layout and behavior. Here are the specific changes:
	1.	AI REASONING AND AI NARRATIVE POSITIONING: The AI Narrative output box must render directly below the AI Reasoning box. Not somewhere else on the page. Directly under it. While Claude is actively reasoning and writing, both boxes are visible and live. Once Claude finishes, both boxes automatically collapse into thin single-line bars that say “AI Reasoning - tap to expand” and “AI Narrative - tap to expand”. User can tap to reopen and read. Collapsed state should be a single line, minimal height, not taking up screen space.
	2.	REMOVE ALL YELLOW WARNING BANNERS: Remove the separate EARNINGS RISK banner, the separate WARNINGS banner, and the separate Events in DTE window banner. All of these are being consolidated into two places: (a) earnings warning goes inline next to the expiration date inside the trade card, and (b) all other warnings go into a collapsed warning bar inside the trade card.
	3.	EARNINGS WARNING INLINE: Inside the trade card, in the EXPIRATION box, add the earnings date right below the expiration date in orange text. Example: “2026-04-24 / 15 DTE” then below it “⚠ EARNINGS APR 24”. This connects the earnings risk to the expiration visually instead of floating in a separate banner.
	4.	WARNINGS BAR: Inside the trade card at the bottom, add a solid yellow (#f5c518) bar with black text that says “⚠ 3 warnings” with “tap to view” on the right. When tapped, it expands to show the warning bullets in black text on yellow background. When collapsed, it is one line. This replaces all the separate yellow/orange banners.
	5.	PROFIT TARGET AND STOP LOSS PROMINENCE: These are currently buried in tiny print. Make them the same visual size and weight as MAX RISK and MAX GAIN. Create a 2-column grid directly below the max risk/max gain/breakeven row. Left column: PROFIT TARGET with the dollar value in green, large font. Right column: STOP LOSS with the dollar value in red, large font. Subtle green border on profit target box, subtle red border on stop loss box. These are the two most actionable numbers on the page.
	6.	TRADE CARD BOX SIZES: The Strategy, Expiration, Contracts, and Net Debit boxes are too tall for the amount of content they contain. Reduce vertical padding by about 40%. Keep the 2x2 grid layout, just compress the height.
	7.	SEND TO ORDER BUTTON: Must sit directly below the trade card and risk overview. Not below the AI Narrative. With the auto-collapse behavior, the button should be visible on screen without scrolling once Claude finishes.
	8.	RISK OVERVIEW CARD: Make it collapsed by default showing one summary line: “RISK OVERVIEW - Loss $213 · POP 86% · R:R 1.3:1 [WARN]”. Tap to expand the full checklist (Pulse Alignment, Risk/Reward, Liquidity, Position Size, DTE/Gamma, Leg Alignment) and the DTE events list. This is below the Send to Order button.
	9.	REVIEW BUTTON ON ORDER TICKET: The Review Options Order button must ALWAYS be active and tappable regardless of risk check status. Never disabled. Never grayed out. This has been requested multiple times. Please fix it.
PAGE ORDER FROM TOP TO BOTTOM:
	•	AI Reasoning (auto-collapses when done)
	•	AI Narrative (directly under reasoning, auto-collapses when done)
	•	Trade card (ticker, strategy, expiration with inline earnings, contracts, net debit, legs with OI, key numbers grid, profit target/stop loss, collapsed warnings bar)

The goal is that after Claude finishes reasoning, the user sees the trade card, key numbers, and Send to Order button on one screen without scrolling.
